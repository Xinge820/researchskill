# Literature Survey

## Principle
A survey answers "what is known and how does it relate to us". It is not a design proposal.

## Rules
- Prefer recent, high-quality primary sources; link every claim.
- If a source was only partly readable, say which part the summary rests on.

## Output
1. One-sentence answer: does it exist, and what does the mainstream do?
2. Approaches grouped by type: principle, key idea or formula, cost, source.
3. Mapping to our problem: what corresponds to what, and where the settings differ.
4. What the literature leaves open and we must test.
5. A suggested order, marked as a suggestion for the user to decide.
