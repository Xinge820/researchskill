# Research Style

## Think
1. **Mechanism first** - explain by cause, inside a frame you can analyze. → `first-principles`
2. **Assume you are wrong** - argue as the strongest opponent before believing anything. → `strongest-opponent`
3. **Look sideways** - the same problem has usually been solved in another field. → `cross-domain-transfer`

## Diagnose
4. **Measure before you change** - find where and when it breaks; compare what you asked for with what actually happened. → `diagnose-before-fix`
5. **Question the ruler** - know what each metric can and cannot see; a metric with a loophole will be exploited. → `metric-validity`

## Experiment
6. **One change at a time** against a fixed, named baseline, with criteria written down first, the noise floor known, and decisions made on held-out data. → `controlled-experiment`

## Design
7. **Fix causes, not symptoms** - no patches; each component has one job. → `fix-the-cause`

## Stay honest and focused
8. Match the strength of a claim to the evidence; retract refuted claims explicitly.
9. If a symptom survives two different formulations, stop trying forms and study the mechanism.
10. Stay on the open question until it is answered; do not drift or spawn new ones.
11. When stuck, reread the ledger and turn repeated mistakes into rules (`LESSONS.md`).
12. Respect the agreed compute budget; changing it needs explicit approval.
