# Proposal Writing

## Structure
Significance → the problems this work addresses → related work (with citations).

## Principles
- Every claim about prior work is backed by a high-quality, recent citation.
- Each stated problem maps one-to-one to a planned piece of work.
- No storytelling: say what each component does and what verifiable gain it brings.
- Keep the research question central; application domains are validation settings, not the topic.
